🍩 Club Rules

To keep Donut Club fun and safe for everyone, please follow these simple rules:

  1. Be Sweet – Treat others with kindness. No bullying, harassment, or hate speech.
  2. No Spamming – Don't flood chat with repeated messages, emoji, or ads.
  3. Keep It PG‑13 – No NSFW content, including images, links, or discussions.
  4. Stay On Topic – Use the correct channels for donut talk, coding help, or off‑topic.
  5. No Self‑Promo Without Permission – Ask a mod before advertising your own server/project.
  6. Respect Privacy – Do not share others' personal information without consent.
  7. Follow Discord's Terms – We're on Discord, so their rules apply too.
  8. Have Fun! – At the end of the day, we're here to enjoy donuts and code.

Breaking rules may result in a warning, mute, or ban. Decisions are final.
