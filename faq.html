❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is Donut Club?
A global community for donut lovers and tech enthusiasts, founded by Wen Zhang in Edmonton, Canada.
How do I join?
Just click the "Join" link in the menu and join our Discord server. It's free and open to everyone.
Is there an age limit?
All ages are welcome! We just ask everyone to follow our Code of Conduct.
Do I need to know how to code?
Not at all. While many members enjoy coding, you're welcome whether you're a programmer or just a donut fan.
How often are events?
We host at least one event per month. Check the Events page for the latest schedule.
Can I suggest a new donut flavour?
Absolutely! Drop a message in our Discord #flavour-suggestions channel or email Wen.
Is Donut Club on social media?
Not yet, but we're planning to launch an Instagram and YouTube channel soon.
Who runs Donut Club?
Wen Zhang (14 years old) with help from volunteer moderators and community members.
How can I report a problem?
Use the Contact form or DM a moderator on Discord.
