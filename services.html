🛎️ Our Services

Donut Club is more than just a chat room. Here's what we offer:

💬 Discord Community

A friendly, moderated server with channels for donut talk, coding help, gaming, and memes.

🌍 Global Chat

Anonymous real‑time chat for quick conversations – no account needed.

📅 Virtual Events

Monthly donut tastings, hackathons, art contests, and game nights.

🍩 Donut Reviews

Read and submit reviews of donuts from around the world in our flavour database.

💻 Coding Resources

Get help with HTML, CSS, JS, Python, and C++ from experienced members (including Wen).

📰 Newsletter (Soon)

Stay updated with club news, event reminders, and exclusive donut content delivered to your inbox.

🛠️ Project Showcase

Promote your own coding projects in the #built-by-us channel and get feedback.

🤝 Collaboration

Find partners for coding projects, donut reviews, or event planning.
