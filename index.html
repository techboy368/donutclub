What is Donut Club?

Donut Club is a worldwide community of donut lovers and tech enthusiasts, founded by Wen Zhang (14, Edmonton, Canada). We host virtual events, share donut reviews, and build awesome coding projects together.

Whether you're here for the sprinkles or the scripts, there's a place for you at the club.
