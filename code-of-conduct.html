📜 Code of Conduct

Donut Club is dedicated to providing a harassment‑free experience for everyone, regardless of age, gender, disability, physical appearance, race, or religion. We do not tolerate harassment in any form.

Expected Behavior

  • Be respectful and considerate in your communication.
  • Use welcoming and inclusive language.
  • Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
  • Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
  • Focus on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable Behavior

  • Harassment, intimidation, or bullying.
  • Hate speech or discriminatory jokes.
  • Posting sexually explicit or violent material.
  • Sharing others' private information without consent.
  • Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate.

Enforcement

Violations may result in a warning, temporary mute, or permanent ban. Moderators have the right to remove any content or member at their discretion.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to a moderator or email wen471942@gmail.com.

This Code of Conduct may be updated at any time.
