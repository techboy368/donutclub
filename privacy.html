🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2025

1. Information We Collect

Donut Club does not directly collect any personal information through this website. The only data we may receive is:

  • Email address if you choose to contact us via the mailto link (handled by your email client).
  • Discord data – when you join our Discord server, Discord's own privacy policy applies.
  • Global Chat messages – anonymous and not stored permanently.

2. How We Use Information

Any email we receive is used solely to respond to your inquiry and is not shared with third parties.

3. Cookies

This website does not use cookies for tracking. Theme preferences are stored locally in your browser (localStorage).

4. Third‑Party Services

We link to external platforms (Discord, GitHub, Codeberg). Please refer to their respective privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Donut Club is open to all ages, but we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe we have such data, please contact us.

6. Changes

We may update this policy occasionally. Check this page for the latest version.
