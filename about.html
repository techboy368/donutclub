About Donut Club

Our Story

Donut Club started as a joke between Wen Zhang and his friends in 2024. While debugging a Python script at 2 AM, Wen grabbed a donut and thought: "What if there was a club for people who love donuts and code as much as I do?" A few Discord messages later, the Donut Club server was born.

Today, Donut Club has members from over 15 countries. We host monthly donut tastings, coding workshops, game nights, and an annual "DonutCon" virtual convention. We believe that sugar and software make a perfect pair.
